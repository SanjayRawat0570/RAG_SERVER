# Retired Laptops

SKU LAP-14-STD was the 14-inch standard laptop and cost 1,250 USD. SKU LAP-15-WS was the 15-inch workstation and cost 2,300 USD. Both were retired in 2023.

# Retired Monitors

SKU MON-27-QHD was the 27-inch QHD monitor and cost 350 USD. SKU MON-22-HD was the 22-inch HD monitor and cost 120 USD.

# Retired Accessories

SKU DCK-USB-A was the USB-A docking station and cost 150 USD. SKU HDS-WR-100 was the wired headset and cost 45 USD.

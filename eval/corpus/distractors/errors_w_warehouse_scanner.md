# Warehouse Scanner Connection Errors

Error W-4012 in the Warehouse Scanner means the local cache is corrupted. Clear the application cache.

Error W-5300 in the Warehouse Scanner means the session cookie is missing. Sign out and sign in again.

Error W-5300 in the Warehouse Scanner means the account has no license assigned. Ask your manager to assign a license.

# Warehouse Scanner Account Errors

Error W-4102 in the Warehouse Scanner means the meeting room is double-booked. Pick another room.

Error W-6007 in the Warehouse Scanner means the record was modified by someone else. Reload the record and repeat the change.

# Warehouse Scanner Data Errors

Error W-7101 in the Warehouse Scanner means the record was modified by someone else. Reload the record and repeat the change.

Error W-7101 in the Warehouse Scanner means the badge has been deactivated. Visit reception to reactivate the badge.

Error W-4120 in the Warehouse Scanner means the request payload is too large. Reduce the size and retry.

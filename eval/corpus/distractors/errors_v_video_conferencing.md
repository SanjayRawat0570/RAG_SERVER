# Video Conferencing Connection Errors

Error V-4021 in the Video Conferencing means the network proxy rejected the connection. Contact the network team.

Error V-4102 in the Video Conferencing means the request payload is too large. Reduce the size and retry.

Error V-4120 in the Video Conferencing means the account has no license assigned. Ask your manager to assign a license.

# Video Conferencing Account Errors

Error V-4102 in the Video Conferencing means the device clock is out of sync. Enable automatic time sync.

Error V-4102 in the Video Conferencing means the upstream service did not answer in time. Retry after a few minutes.

Error V-7110 in the Video Conferencing means the request payload is too large. Reduce the size and retry.

# Video Conferencing Data Errors

Error V-4012 in the Video Conferencing means the user lacks the required role. Request the role in the access portal.

Error V-4102 in the Video Conferencing means the barcode could not be read. Clean the scanner window.

Error V-7101 in the Video Conferencing means the device clock is out of sync. Enable automatic time sync.

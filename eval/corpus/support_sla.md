# Standard Support

Standard support includes email support from Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET. The first response time is 8 business hours. Standard customers get an uptime commitment of 99.5%.

# Premium Support

Premium support includes phone, chat and email support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The first response time is 1 hour. Premium customers get an uptime commitment of 99.9% and a dedicated account manager.

# Support Pricing

Premium support costs 15% of the annual contract value. Standard support is included at no extra cost.

# Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee.

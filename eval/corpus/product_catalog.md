# Laptops

SKU LAP-14-PRO is the 14-inch ProBook laptop. It costs 1,450 USD and is the standard laptop for engineers.

SKU LAP-13-AIR is the 13-inch AirBook laptop. It costs 1,100 USD and is the standard laptop for all other roles.

# Monitors

SKU MON-27-4K is the 27-inch 4K monitor and costs 420 USD.

SKU MON-24-FHD is the 24-inch Full HD monitor and costs 180 USD.

# Accessories

SKU DCK-USB-C is the USB-C docking station and costs 210 USD.

SKU HDS-BT-200 is the Bluetooth headset and costs 95 USD.

# Ordering Hardware

Hardware is ordered through the IT portal. Orders above 2,000 USD in total require manager approval.

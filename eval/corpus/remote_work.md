# Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

# Home Office Stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD to buy a desk, chair or monitor.

# Laptops

Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately.

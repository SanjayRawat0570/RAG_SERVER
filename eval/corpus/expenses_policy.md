# Travel Expenses

Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.

The daily meal allowance while travelling is capped at 60 USD.

# Expense Claims

Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.

# Approvals

Expenses above 500 USD require manager approval. Expenses above 5,000 USD also require approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

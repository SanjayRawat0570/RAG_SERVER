# Health Insurance Plans

Employees can choose between two health insurance plans: the Essential plan and the Plus plan.

# Essential Plan

The Essential plan is fully paid by the company. Its annual deductible is 1,200 USD. The Essential plan does not include vision coverage.

# Plus Plan

The Plus plan costs employees 45 USD per month. Its annual deductible is 400 USD. The Plus plan includes vision coverage of up to 200 USD per year for glasses or contact lenses.

# Enrollment

Open enrollment runs from 1 November to 30 November each year. New employees must choose a plan within 30 days of their start date.

# Other Benefits

Employees receive a gym reimbursement of 30 USD per month. Life insurance coverage equals two times annual salary.

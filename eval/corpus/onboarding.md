# First Day

New employees collect their laptop and YubiKey at the IT desk at 09:00 on their first day. Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team.

# Equipment for New Hires

Engineers receive the LAP-14-PRO laptop. All other new employees receive the LAP-13-AIR laptop.

# Onboarding Training

New employees must complete security awareness training within 14 days of their start date.

# Probation

The probation period is 6 months. The notice period during probation is 2 weeks.

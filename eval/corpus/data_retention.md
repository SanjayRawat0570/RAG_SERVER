# Customer Data

Customer account data is deleted 90 days after the contract ends. Invoices are kept for 10 years to meet tax law.

# Logs

Application logs are kept for 30 days. Security audit logs are kept for 1 year.

# Backups

Backups are retained for 35 days and are encrypted with AES-256.

# Employee Records

Employee records are kept for 6 years after employment ends.

# Data Subject Requests

GDPR data subject requests must be answered within 30 days. The data protection officer is Sofia Marin, who can be reached at dpo@example.com.

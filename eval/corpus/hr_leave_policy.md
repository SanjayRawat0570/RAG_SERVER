# Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.

Part-time staff receive annual leave pro rata, calculated from their contracted weekly hours. A part-time employee working 3 days a week receives 15 days of annual leave.

Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March.

# Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

# Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

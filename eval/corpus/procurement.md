# Purchase Orders

Every purchase above 1,000 USD needs a purchase order. Purchase order numbers use the format PO-YYYY-NNNNN, for example PO-2025-00418. Purchases above 10,000 USD require three competing quotes.

# Approved Vendors

VND-0042 is CloudNorth, the hosting provider. VND-0117 is PrintWorks, the office printing supplier. VND-0230 is ShieldSec, the penetration testing firm.

# Vendor Security Review

Any vendor that will process customer data must pass a security review by the Security team before the contract is signed.

# Payment Terms

Vendor invoices are paid on net 45 day terms.

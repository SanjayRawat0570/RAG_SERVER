# Travel Policy 2023

This travel policy applied from 1 January 2023 until 31 December 2024. It was replaced by the Travel Expenses policy that is effective from 1 January 2025.

# Meals and Hotels (2023)

Under the 2023 travel policy, the daily meal allowance was 45 USD. Hotel stays were capped at 150 USD per night.

# Flights (2023)

Under the 2023 travel policy, economy class was required for flights shorter than 8 hours.
